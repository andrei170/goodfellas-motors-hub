The Website Audit (Grade: D)

+

Your site works for visitors, but it is leaking financing leads. What we found:

StrongLive inventory with real vehicle pages. Your stock is online with search-friendly URLs. This is the bright spot to build on.
GapThe financing funnel is generic. A basic credit-app form, not a real "see what you qualify for" pre-qualification, which is the whole game for a financing-led lot.
SplitSpanish is on a separate domain. Your bilingual buyers are split onto a second site, which weakens both.
WeakLead capture is scattered across pages with several different phone numbers and no chat.
PlatformThe DealerCenter builder caps you on speed, control, and SEO. The "404" you may have seen is a bot-block screen, not a dead page, but worth confirming.
3

The SEO Audit (Grade: D)

+

Good bones on inventory, weak everywhere it counts for financing:

StrongVehicle pages are indexable and keyword-structured. Good foundation.
RiskA bot wall may be blocking Google. Your robots and sitemap return a block screen. If Google is challenged too, your pages may not be getting crawled. Worth checking right away.
GapNo proper schema. Dealer and vehicle markup is almost certainly missing, so you forfeit richer search results.
GapYou do not own the money searches. "Bad credit car loans" and "buy here pay here" in the metro are exactly what you should rank for, and there is no sign you are targeting them.
WeakYour listings disagree. Several phone numbers and a wrong address floating in the data hurt local trust.
